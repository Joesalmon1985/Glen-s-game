# Scene 4 — Door Procedure

**Ask:** stay away from the door so they can enter.

**If comply:** door opens; cautious escort. **If refuse:** repeat, clearer gestures, more staff, then controlled opening and physical move. **If attack:** resolve the actual weak/outnumbered attempt; narrator distinguishes intention vs action. Institution progresses regardless.

**Transition out:** Corridor, then wash. Lead with leaving the cell.
