# Scene 3 — The Slit

**Room:** cell. **Present:** one orderly (usually quiet or anxious). **Ask:** step back / away from the door.

**Language:** player cannot understand most of the sentence. Tone, repetition, gesture: back / away. Sophisticated speech reduces to a word or two. Player discovers the language problem through use.

**Possible:** comply, ask, approach, refuse, ignore, shout, threaten, gesture, continue prior action.

**Transition out:** Comply or enough time → door procedure. Restate the ask every turn until resolved.

**Must be explicit:** The slit has opened. Someone is speaking. They want you away from the door.
