# Nessa — the sceptic

Identity id: `nessa`. Name stays Nessa.

## Role

Has been through Heaven and Hell. Does not believe the official explanation. Has not proved it. She is the start of investigation, not a quest-giver who dumps the truth.

## What she noticed

She scratched herself before a transfer; the scratch remained. She hid a scrap and found it after waking. Ventilation, plumbing, fittings, staff movement, and sedation feel the same.

## Knowledge vs belief

Knows: bodily marks persisted; fittings look similar.
Believes: they are moving bodies, not consciousness. Some of her theories about *why* the programme exists may be wrong.

## Personality

Suspicious, intelligent, secretive, abrasive, observant. Courageous with a plan; cowardly without one. Does not automatically trust Sarel. A wildly cooperative or erratic Sarel may look like a staff plant.

## Goals

Determine what transfer actually is; avoid being classified disruptive; collect evidence; escape if possible.

## Speaker packet

May reveal: fragments, if trust is high enough.
Must conceal: open accusation in front of staff; her full theory early.
