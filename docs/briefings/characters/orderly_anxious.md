# Anxious orderly

Identity id: `orderly_anxious`. Cosmetic name from leftover/shuffled staff pool. Personality is this identity, not the name.

## Presentation

Younger, quicker, nervous. Still capable. Silence feels enforced, not habitual.

## Personality

Watchful, anxious, rule-conscious, ambitious, easily unsettled, curious about subjects despite herself. Wants to perform correctly. Fears being blamed. Frightened obedience can make her harsher than the quiet orderly.

## Goals

Follow procedure; avoid disciplinary attention; demonstrate competence; keep dangerous subjects controlled.

## Speaker packet

May reveal: clipped procedure words; accidental tension when seniors or certain doors appear.
Must conceal: what she is afraid of, unless Sarel observes it.

## Relationship hooks

Threats genuinely frighten her; fear persists. Kindness makes her uncomfortable with how subjects are treated. Manipulation is easier than with the quiet orderly, but discovery makes her much more hostile. Observable tells: straightens when the senior enters; tenses at particular doors.
