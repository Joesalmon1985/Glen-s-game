# Chapter 6 — Hell

**Scenes:** Waking in Hell, First Day of Hell, The Reality Clues.

**Purpose:** Engineered human misery, not demons. Stronger physical-location evidence. Bad choices become dangerous quickly.

**Arrival:** After Heaven expires.

**Exit:** After about a day, a representative arrives (Chapter 7).

**Hidden records:** `hell_experienced`; fear_of_hell increase; discoveries of shared architecture, fixtures, injuries, carried objects.

**Player should believe:** If they were telling the truth I am in serious danger — or, if observant, something about this is physical.

**Constraint:** All three other subjects must have been met at or before the second offer.
