# Fighting Fantasy rules (Deathtrap Dungeon pack)

## Adventure Sheet
- **Skill** — combat and skill tests. Initial: 1d6+6.
- **Stamina** — hit points. Initial: 2d6+12. At 0 you are dead.
- **Luck** — Test your Luck; usually reduce Luck by 1 after a test. Initial: 1d6+6.

## Combat
1. Roll 2d6 + your Skill for Attack Strength.
2. Roll 2d6 + enemy Skill for their Attack Strength.
3. Higher wins the round: loser loses 2 Stamina (unless a special rule says otherwise).
4. Ties: no damage.
5. Continue until one side reaches 0 Stamina.

## Test your Luck
Roll 2d6. If the total is **less than or equal to** current Luck, you are Lucky; otherwise Unlucky. Then reduce Luck by 1 (unless a passage says otherwise).

## Test your Skill
Roll 2d6. If the total is less than or equal to Skill, you succeed.

## Provisions
Eating a provision restores 4 Stamina (not above Initial Stamina), unless a passage says otherwise.

## Potions
One potion at start. Drinking restores the named score to its Initial value (Fortune also raises Initial Luck by 1 in classic rules).
